# Marketing Campaign Assistant

An intelligent AI-powered marketing campaign assistant that automatically processes your marketing campaign idea and turn it into a digital marketing content (currently limited to instagram, youtube ad).

[![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3.8+-blue.svg)](https://python.org)
[![OpenAI](https://img.shields.io/badge/OpenAI-GPT--4o--mini-green.svg)](https://openai.com)

## 🎯 What It Does

The Marketing Campaign Assistant uses advanced AI reasoning to:

- **Generate full campaign brief (messaging, creative hooks, personas, channels)** from human inputs
- **Polished, on-brand version of campaign concepts** based on campaign brief and company brand guidelide
- **Create content** for instagram post and reels
- **Create content** for youtube ads
- **Automaticaly post content** to instagram and youtube
